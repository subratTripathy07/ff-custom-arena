Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an official Free Fire website?

No. FF Custom Arena is an independent, community-run platform and is not affiliated with Garena.

How do I join a tournament?

Register an account, create or join a team, then register your team for an open tournament.

When is the room ID released?

Room details are released shortly before match start and are visible only to registered teams.
